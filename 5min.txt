# 标题突出青海从送绿水、送绿电到送绿算的实践主线。
Title: From Sending Green Water and Green Power to Delivering Green Computing: Qinghai's Practice in China's Ecological Progress

# 开场直接说明代表中国发言，并点明本稿聚焦青海的三重绿色贡献。
Hello everyone. I am honored to represent China in today's discussion on environmental conservation. Today, I would like to introduce Qinghai, a highland province whose story moves from sending green water, to transmitting green power, to delivering green computing. Through this case, we can see what tangible changes China's environmental initiatives have brought, and how China is turning ecological responsibility into long-term development strength.

# 本段自然引出青海如何把特殊生态地位转化为面向全国的绿色贡献。
Qinghai's significance begins with protection, but it does not end there. At the source of the Yangtze River, the Yellow River, and the Lancang River, this province is known as China's "Water Tower." It also possesses abundant sunlight, powerful wind, broad land, and growing digital infrastructure. What begins on this plateau does not remain on this plateau. Its water flows downstream, its electricity crosses provinces, and its computing power now supports the digital economy. Qinghai therefore offers not only a story of conservation, but a story of service to the nation.

# 本段概括青海从“三好”到“三绿”的发展逻辑。
This is the deeper meaning of Qinghai's transformation from "three natural strengths" to "three green contributions." In the past, people described Qinghai as a place of good water, good sunlight, and good wind. Today, these advantages are being reshaped into green water, green power, and green computing. This is not a decorative phrase. It is a development logic: protect the source, convert the resource, serve the whole country. In Qinghai, ecology is not treated as a burden outside development; it becomes the foundation from which a new development path rises.

# 本段聚焦“送绿水”，用数据说明青海向下游输送清洁水源。
At the head of this contribution stands green water. The Sanjiangyuan region, the source area of the three great rivers, now conserves about 40.895 billion cubic meters of water each year and sends roughly 60 to 90 billion cubic meters of source water downstream annually. For many years, Qinghai's outbound surface water has remained above 60 billion cubic meters. The main streams of the Yellow River, the Yangtze River, and the Lancang River in Qinghai maintain water quality at Grade II or above. This means that Qinghai is not only keeping its own rivers clear; it is sending clean water across basins, supporting ecological security and daily life for hundreds of millions of people downstream.

# 本段说明“送绿水”背后的治理能力，但避免把重心停留在生态修复。
Behind this steady flow stands a durable system of governance. Qinghai has built a river and lake chief system from the provincial level down to the village level, giving more than 3,500 rivers and over 500 lakes dedicated guardians. In Sanjiangyuan National Park, 17,211 herders serve as certified ecological conservation workers. Across the province, ecological public-service positions exceed 140,000. Green water, in this sense, is not a natural accident. It is the result of long-term stewardship, institutional responsibility, and a public system capable of guarding the river sources while keeping people connected to development.

# 本段聚焦“送绿电”，说明青海清洁能源基础和塔拉滩样本。
From water, Qinghai's contribution expands into green power. Its clean energy installed capacity had exceeded 73 million kilowatts by October 2025, accounting for more than 93 percent of the province's total power capacity. In Gonghe County, the Talatan area has become a photovoltaic landscape covering about 350 square kilometers. Solar panels stretch across the plateau like a blue ocean; beneath them, grass grows and sheep graze. This image carries a larger message: Qinghai is not choosing between energy production and ecological improvement. It is building an energy system in which the two can advance together.

# 本段强调青海绿电外送，突出从资源地到国家清洁能源高地的跃升。
Transmission gives this green power national reach. The Qinghai-Henan plus-minus 800 kilovolt ultra-high-voltage direct-current project, placed into operation in 2020, stretches 1,587 kilometers from Qinghai to Henan. Over five years, it has transmitted more than 80 billion kilowatt-hours of green electricity. Qinghai's green power now reaches 16 provincial-level regions, and since the beginning of the 14th Five-Year Plan period, cumulative outbound green electricity has exceeded 110 billion kilowatt-hours. In 2024 alone, clean energy investment in Qinghai reached 40.6 billion yuan. Step by step, Qinghai is becoming a clean-energy highland powering development far beyond its borders.

# 本段聚焦“送绿算”，说明绿电进一步转化为数字时代的算力服务。
From power, the story advances toward green computing. As artificial intelligence, cloud services, industrial internet, and scientific research expand, computing power is becoming a new engine of productivity. Yet computing requires enormous electricity. If that electricity is carbon-intensive, the digital future will carry an ecological debt. Qinghai offers a different route: use green electricity to support green computing. In this way, the province does not simply send power through cables; it also transforms electricity into data capacity, digital intelligence, and services for regions that need low-carbon computing.

# 本段用绿色算力数据增强论据，突出算电协同实践。
The progress is concrete. The China Unicom Sanjiangyuan National Big Data Base in Xining has attracted about 2.6 billion yuan in investment. Its computing capacity has reached around 9,000 petaflops and is expected to rise to about 13,000 petaflops. Qinghai's clean energy and green computing dispatch center, launched in June 2024, monitors power data from 559 clean energy stations and carbon-emission curves from 912 energy-consuming enterprises. By September, Qinghai had built about 190,000 standard racks, and its computing scale had grown nearly fortyfold within a year. Qinghai is turning watts into bits, green electricity into green computing, and regional resources into national digital capacity.

# 本段以介绍式总结环保举措带来的实际变化，突出水、电、算的输出。
Through these changes, China's environmental initiatives become visible in concrete flows of value. Clean water moves from river sources to downstream regions. Green electricity travels across 1,587 kilometers of ultra-high-voltage lines. Green computing emerges from data centers powered by renewable energy. These are not isolated achievements. They reshape resource flows, energy structures, industrial geography, and the relationship between China's western highlands and the country's broader modernization.

# 本段以隐含方式说明短期发展与长期可持续的平衡，强调“守护与转化”。
This is also where Qinghai reveals China's approach to balancing development and sustainability. The province protects the river sources, because without water security there can be no lasting development. It develops clean energy, because growth needs power but the future needs low carbon. It builds green computing, because digital progress must not be separated from climate responsibility. The balance is neither passive preservation nor blind expansion. It is disciplined conversion: ecological value becoming economic strength, and local resources becoming national service.

# 结尾贴近背景，号召听众从青海实践理解中国绿色发展的现实路径。
To close, Qinghai's practice gives us a powerful introduction to China's ecological progress. From sending green water, to transmitting green power, to delivering green computing, Qinghai shows that environmental initiatives can produce visible, measurable, and shareable results. It reminds us that sustainability is not a distant ideal; it is a system of rivers, grids, data centers, industries, and people working in the same direction. As we continue this discussion, let us look at Qinghai not only as China's Water Tower, but also as a green-energy highland and a rising green-computing hub. Its message is clear: the future belongs to development that can nourish the land, power the nation, and serve the digital age without betraying the planet.
