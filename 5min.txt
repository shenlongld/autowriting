# 标题概括5分钟版聚焦青海绿色实践，服务中国生态进步主题。
Title: Qinghai's Green Practice: From China's Water Tower to a Future of Green Development

# 开场参照课堂讨论语境，直接说明代表中国发言并点明青海主题。
Hello everyone. I am honored to represent China in today's discussion on environmental conservation. Today, I would like to introduce Qinghai, a province on the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau and a vital window into China's ecological progress. Through Qinghai's transformation from green water to green power and then to green computing, we can see what tangible changes China's environmental initiatives have brought, and how China is seeking to balance short-term development with long-term sustainability.

# 本段从文明和生态安全高度说明青海的重要性。
Qinghai is not only a place on a map. It is the birthplace of the Yangtze River, the Yellow River, and the Lancang River. It is known as China's "Water Tower," because the water that begins on this highland travels across mountains and plains, nourishing ecosystems, farms, cities, and industries far downstream. The issue Qinghai once faced was serious: some river source areas suffered from shrinking lakes, grassland degradation, ecological fragility, and the pressure of disorderly development. The reason this mattered was larger than Qinghai itself. When the headwaters are protected, downstream security is strengthened; when the grasslands recover, biodiversity and climate resilience recover with them. That is why Qinghai became a crucial testing ground for China's ecological civilization.

# 本段概括青海从自然禀赋到绿色贡献的发展逻辑。
The story of Qinghai can be understood as a grand movement from natural endowment to national contribution. In the past, people described Qinghai through three strengths: good water, good sunlight, and good wind. Today, those strengths are being transformed into three green contributions: green water, green power, and green computing. This change is not merely a change of words. It is a change of development philosophy. Nature provides the foundation, but governance, technology, and responsibility turn that foundation into lasting progress.

# 本段用数据说明“绿水”建设和三江源生态保护成果。
The transformation begins with green water. Since the launch of major protection and restoration programs in Sanjiangyuan, and since China's first national park system pilot began there in 2016, Qinghai has advanced systematic protection of mountains, rivers, forests, lakes, grasslands, and deserts. Today, the Sanjiangyuan region conserves about 40.895 billion cubic meters of water each year and sends roughly 60 to 90 billion cubic meters of source water downstream annually. The main streams of the Yellow River, the Yangtze River, and the Lancang River in Qinghai maintain water quality at Grade II or above. These are not distant statistics. They mean clearer rivers, safer water systems, stronger ecosystems, and a more secure ecological foundation for hundreds of millions of people.

# 本段说明制度和群众参与如何让生态保护真正落地。
This progress did not come from slogans alone. It came from institutions, responsibilities, and people. Qinghai has established a river and lake chief system from the provincial level down to the village level. More than 3,500 rivers and over 500 lakes have dedicated guardians. In Sanjiangyuan National Park, 17,211 herders serve as certified ecological conservation workers, and across the province there are more than 140,000 ecological public-service positions. In this model, local people are not passive observers of conservation. They are protectors of the rivers, guardians of the grasslands, and beneficiaries of green development.

# 本段用塔拉滩和能源数据说明“绿电”带来的生态与产业变化。
The second transformation is green power. Qinghai has abundant sunlight, strong wind, rich water resources, and vast land suitable for clean energy development. By October 2025, its clean energy installed capacity had exceeded 73 million kilowatts, accounting for more than 93 percent of the province's total power capacity. In Gonghe County, the Talatan area was once a barren land of wind and sand. Today, it has become a photovoltaic landscape covering about 350 square kilometers. Solar panels stretch like a blue ocean across the plateau. They reduce wind speed, slow water evaporation from the soil, allow grass to return, and create the remarkable scene of electricity above and grazing below. Here, clean energy is not built at the expense of ecology; it becomes a force that helps restore it.

# 本段说明青海绿电如何通过外送工程服务国家低碳转型。
Green power also allows Qinghai to serve the wider nation. The Qinghai-Henan plus-minus 800 kilovolt ultra-high-voltage direct-current project, put into operation in 2020, stretches 1,587 kilometers from Qinghai to Henan. Over five years, it has transmitted more than 80 billion kilowatt-hours of green electricity. Qinghai's green power now reaches 16 provincial-level regions, and since the beginning of the 14th Five-Year Plan period, cumulative outbound green electricity has exceeded 110 billion kilowatt-hours. In 2024 alone, clean energy investment in Qinghai reached 40.6 billion yuan. A province once defined mainly by ecological responsibility is now also becoming a national clean-energy highland.

# 本段引出“绿算”，说明数字时代的绿色发展新命题。
The third transformation is green computing. In the digital age, artificial intelligence, cloud services, scientific research, and industrial platforms all require vast computing power, and computing power requires vast electricity. If that electricity is carbon-intensive, digital progress will carry a heavy ecological cost. Qinghai is offering another answer: use green electricity to power green computing. In this way, watts become bits, clean energy becomes digital intelligence, and ecological advantage becomes a new form of productive strength.

# 本段用具体数据说明青海绿色算力和算电协同进展。
The progress is already visible. The China Unicom Sanjiangyuan National Big Data Base in Xining has attracted about 2.6 billion yuan in investment. Its computing capacity has reached around 9,000 petaflops and is expected to rise to about 13,000 petaflops. Qinghai's clean energy and green computing dispatch center, launched in June 2024, monitors power data from 559 clean energy stations and carbon-emission curves from 912 energy-consuming enterprises. By September, Qinghai had built about 190,000 standard racks, and its computing scale had grown nearly fortyfold within a year. This is green development entering the frontier of the digital economy.

# 本段集中回答中国环保举措带来的实际变化。
What tangible changes have China's environmental initiatives brought? In Qinghai, the answer is clear. Rivers are cleaner. Grasslands are recovering. Ecological guardians have stable roles and income. Solar power has transformed barren land into productive green space. Ultra-high-voltage lines carry clean electricity across regions. Green computing is turning renewable energy into new industrial capacity. These changes reshape landscapes, livelihoods, energy structures, and the very meaning of development.

# 本段回答如何平衡短期发展和长期可持续。
How does China balance short-term development with long-term sustainability? Qinghai shows that the answer is not to abandon development, but to raise its standard. Development that damages river sources is not true development. Growth that exhausts grasslands is not true prosperity. Industry that leaves ecological debt is not true modernization. Qinghai protects what must be protected through national parks, ecological red lines, river governance, and public stewardship. It develops what should be developed through clean energy, eco-tourism, organic agriculture and animal husbandry, and green computing.

# 本段以木里煤田说明放弃短期利益、转向长期责任的必要性。
The transformation of the Muli coalfield gives this lesson moral weight. The area once held rich coal resources and brought short-term fiscal gains, but disorderly exploitation damaged the fragile ecology of the southern Qilian Mountains. In 2020, local mining enterprises were shut down, and restoration became a priority. Today, places such as Tianjun are turning toward ecological animal husbandry and tourism, with tourism revenue reaching 212 million yuan in 2024. This choice shows that long-term responsibility sometimes requires the courage to step away from immediate profit.

# 结尾贴近background，号召听众理解青海实践并承担共同责任。
To close, Qinghai is not an isolated example; it is a concentrated expression of China's ecological progress. From green water to green power to green computing, it shows that environmental initiatives can be measured in cleaner rivers, restored grasslands, lower-carbon energy, stronger industries, and better livelihoods. It also reminds us that sustainability is not a barrier to development, but the foundation of development worthy of the future. As we continue this discussion, let us look carefully at Qinghai's practice and carry forward its central message: the rivers we protect, the energy we choose, and the technologies we build will shape not only today's growth, but tomorrow's shared destiny.
