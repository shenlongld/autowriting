# 标题突出青海从送绿水、送绿电到送绿算的实践主线。
Title: From Sending Green Water and Green Power to Delivering Green Computing: Qinghai's Practice in China's Ecological Progress

# 开场直接说明代表中国发言，并点明本稿聚焦青海的三重绿色贡献。
Hello everyone. I am honored to represent China in today's discussion on environmental conservation. Today, I would like to introduce Qinghai, a highland province whose story moves from sending green water, to transmitting green power, to delivering green computing. Through Qinghai, China's environmental initiatives become a living practice: ecological responsibility is transformed into national service, industrial renewal, and long-term development strength.

# 本段自然引出青海如何把特殊生态地位转化为面向全国的绿色贡献。
Qinghai's significance rises from protection into contribution. At the source of the Yangtze River, the Yellow River, and the Lancang River, this province is known as China's "Water Tower." It also possesses abundant sunlight, powerful wind, broad land, and growing digital infrastructure. What begins on this plateau travels outward with national consequence. Its water flows downstream, its electricity crosses provinces, and its computing power now supports the digital economy. Qinghai therefore stands as a story of conservation elevated into service to the nation.

# 本段概括青海从“三好”到“三绿”的发展逻辑。
From this highland has emerged a transformation from "three natural strengths" to "three green contributions." In the past, people described Qinghai as a place of good water, good sunlight, and good wind. Today, these advantages are being reshaped into green water, green power, and green computing. The phrase carries a clear development logic: protect the source, convert the resource, serve the whole country. In Qinghai, ecology becomes the foundation from which a new development path rises.

# 本段聚焦“送绿水”，用数据说明青海向下游输送清洁水源。
Green water flows first from the source. The Sanjiangyuan region, the source area of the three great rivers, now conserves about 40.895 billion cubic meters of water each year and sends roughly 60 to 90 billion cubic meters of source water downstream annually. For many years, Qinghai's outbound surface water has remained above 60 billion cubic meters. The main streams of the Yellow River, the Yangtze River, and the Lancang River in Qinghai maintain water quality at Grade II or above. From this highland, clear water crosses basins, strengthens ecological security, and supports daily life for hundreds of millions of people downstream.

# 本段说明“送绿水”背后的治理能力，但避免把重心停留在生态修复。
Behind this steady flow stands a durable system of governance. Qinghai has built a river and lake chief system from the provincial level down to the village level, giving more than 3,500 rivers and over 500 lakes dedicated guardians. In Sanjiangyuan National Park, 17,211 herders serve as certified ecological conservation workers. Across the province, ecological public-service positions exceed 140,000. Green water is sustained by long-term stewardship, institutional responsibility, and a public system capable of guarding the river sources while keeping people connected to development.

# 本段聚焦“送绿电”，说明青海清洁能源基础和塔拉滩样本。
Across the plateau, green power has become another great current. Qinghai's clean energy installed capacity had exceeded 73 million kilowatts by October 2025, accounting for more than 93 percent of the province's total power capacity. In Gonghe County, the Talatan area has become a photovoltaic landscape covering about 350 square kilometers. Solar panels stretch across the plateau like a blue ocean; beneath them, grass grows and sheep graze. This image carries a larger message: energy production and ecological improvement can advance on the same land, under the same sun, toward the same future.

# 本段强调青海绿电外送，突出从资源地到国家清洁能源高地的跃升。
Through vast transmission corridors, this green power gains national reach. The Qinghai-Henan plus-minus 800 kilovolt ultra-high-voltage direct-current project, placed into operation in 2020, stretches 1,587 kilometers from Qinghai to Henan. Over five years, it has transmitted more than 80 billion kilowatt-hours of green electricity. Qinghai's green power now reaches 16 provincial-level regions, and since the beginning of the 14th Five-Year Plan period, cumulative outbound green electricity has exceeded 110 billion kilowatt-hours. In 2024 alone, clean energy investment in Qinghai reached 40.6 billion yuan. Across these routes of power, Qinghai is emerging as a clean-energy highland whose influence extends far beyond its borders.

# 本段聚焦“送绿算”，说明绿电进一步转化为数字时代的算力服务。
On the foundation of green power, Qinghai is entering the frontier of green computing. As artificial intelligence, cloud services, industrial internet, and scientific research expand, computing power is becoming a new engine of productivity. This rise places enormous demand on electricity, and carbon-intensive power would burden the digital future with ecological debt. Qinghai offers a higher route: green electricity supporting green computing. In this way, the province transforms electricity into data capacity, digital intelligence, and services for regions that need low-carbon computing.

# 本段用绿色算力数据增强论据，突出算电协同实践。
In Xining, the China Unicom Sanjiangyuan National Big Data Base has attracted about 2.6 billion yuan in investment. Its computing capacity has reached around 9,000 petaflops and is expected to rise to about 13,000 petaflops. Qinghai's clean energy and green computing dispatch center, launched in June 2024, monitors power data from 559 clean energy stations and carbon-emission curves from 912 energy-consuming enterprises. By September, Qinghai had built about 190,000 standard racks, and its computing scale had grown nearly fortyfold within a year. Qinghai is turning watts into bits, green electricity into green computing, and regional resources into national digital capacity.

# 本段以介绍式总结环保举措带来的实际变化，突出水、电、算的输出。
Clean water, green electricity, and green computing make China's environmental initiatives visible in concrete flows of value. Clean water moves from river sources to downstream regions. Green electricity travels across 1,587 kilometers of ultra-high-voltage lines. Green computing emerges from data centers powered by renewable energy. Together, they reshape resource flows, energy structures, industrial geography, and the relationship between China's western highlands and the country's broader modernization.

# 本段以隐含方式说明短期发展与长期可持续的平衡，强调“守护与转化”。
Qinghai's practice also illuminates China's approach to balancing development and sustainability. The balance is not achieved by slowing progress, nor by pursuing growth at any ecological cost; it is achieved by changing the quality of growth itself. The province protects the river sources, because water security anchors lasting development. It develops clean energy, because growth requires power and the future requires low carbon. It builds green computing, because digital progress must stand with climate responsibility. In this way, short-term development gains a cleaner engine, while long-term sustainability gains a stronger economic foundation. The balance is disciplined conversion: ecological value becoming economic strength, and local resources becoming national service.

# 结尾贴近背景，号召听众从青海实践理解中国绿色发展的现实路径。
Qinghai's practice gives us a powerful introduction to China's ecological progress. From sending green water, to transmitting green power, to delivering green computing, Qinghai shows that environmental initiatives can produce visible, measurable, and shareable results. Sustainability here becomes a working system of rivers, grids, data centers, industries, and people moving in the same direction. As we continue this discussion, let us look at Qinghai as China's Water Tower, a green-energy highland, and a rising green-computing hub. Its message is clear: the future belongs to development that can nourish the land, power the nation, and serve the digital age without betraying the planet.
