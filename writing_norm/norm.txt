Making a PowerPoint presentation on China's ecological progress

With this background, it is now your turn to make a PowerPoint presentation to respond to the questions raised in Scenario:
What tangible changes have China's new environmental initiatives brought about?
How has China balanced short-term gains with long-term sustainability?

Your presentation should answer two key questions:
What tangible changes have China's environmental initiatives brought about?
How has China balanced short-term development with long-term sustainability?

*Speaking skill
Here are some tips for making a compelling PowerPoint presentation:

• Use visuals effectively. Keep slides simple and visually appealing. Use a clean layout, consistent fonts, and a limited color scheme. Avoid overcrowding slides with text – stick to bullet points or key phrases. Use images and visualized data to make your message easier to understand.
• Engage the audience. Use light-hearted storytelling, relatable examples, or interactive questions to connect with your audience. Maintain eye contact, speak clearly, and use natural gestures. Avoid reading directly from the slides; instead, use them as prompts to elaborate on your points.
• Practice for smooth delivery. Rehearse your presentation and stay within the time limit. Practice transitions between slides and anticipate potential questions from the audience.

*Structure your presentation
After developing your ideas, organize them into a clear structure. The following is a suggested outline.

• Introduction
– Briefly introduce yourself and your topic.
– Express appreciation to your audience for their engagement.

• Body
– Highlight notable outcomes of China's environmental initiatives, such as eco-environmental restoration, pollution prevention and reduction, as well as green industry development.
– Discuss short-term gains versus long-term sustainability by showcasing policies and community efforts focused on long-term ecological sustainability.
– Provide examples of global environmental efforts, emphasizing global collaboration, long-term benefits, and fostering a sense of global community.

• Conclusion
– Summarize your key points and emphasize shared responsibility to motivate everyone to take action to protect the environment.
– Encourage questions and further discussion.

Enhance your language
• Stating long-term conservation policies or initiatives
e.g., To create a pleasant living environment, China has launched a comprehensive campaign to protect blue skies, clear waters, and clean land.
