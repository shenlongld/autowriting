# 短期发展与长期可持续的平衡，强调“守护与转化”。
Qinghai's practice also reveals how China seeks to balance development and sustainability. The balance is not to slow progress, nor to pursue growth at ecological cost, but to improve the quality of growth itself. Qinghai protects river sources to secure long-term water safety, develops clean energy to power low-carbon growth, and builds green computing to support digital progress responsibly. In this way, short-term development gains a cleaner engine, while long-term sustainability gains a stronger economic foundation.

# 结尾大气收束，号召听众从青海实践理解中国绿色发展的现实路径。
Qinghai offers a concrete window into China's ecological progress. From sending green water, to transmitting green power, to delivering green computing, it shows that environmental initiatives can produce visible, measurable and shareable results. Its message is clear: the future belongs to development that can nourish the land, power the nation, and serve the digital age without betraying the planet.
