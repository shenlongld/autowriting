# 本段聚焦“送绿算”，说明绿电进一步转化为数字时代的算力服务。
On the foundation of green power, Qinghai is advancing toward green computing. As artificial intelligence, cloud services and scientific research expand, computing power has become a strategic form of productivity, but it also requires vast electricity. Qinghai's answer is to support data centers with renewable energy, transforming clean power into low-carbon computing capacity for national development.

# 本段用绿色算力数据增强论据，突出算电协同实践。
In Xining, the China Unicom Sanjiangyuan National Big Data Base has attracted about 2.6 billion yuan in investment. Its computing capacity has reached around 9,000 petaflops and is expected to rise to about 13,000 petaflops. Qinghai's clean energy and green computing dispatch center, launched in June 2024, monitors power data from 559 clean energy stations and carbon-emission curves from 912 energy-consuming enterprises. By September, Qinghai had built about 190,000 standard racks, and its computing scale had grown nearly fortyfold within a year. In this process, watts are converted into bits, and renewable resources are converted into digital capacity.

# 本段以介绍式总结环保举措带来的实际变化，突出水、电、算的输出。
Clean water, green electricity and green computing together reveal the tangible results of China's environmental initiatives. Water flows from the river sources to downstream regions; electricity travels through 1,587 kilometers of ultra-high-voltage lines; computing power rises from data centers supported by renewable energy. Qinghai is therefore not only a protected plateau, but also a concrete example of ecological value becoming development capacity.
